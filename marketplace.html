The workspace

Six things that stop falling through the cracks

Winning the brief is the visible part of the job. Everything after it is where events quietly go wrong.

Budgets that reconcile

Allocate by category, land every quote and payment against a line, and watch allocated, spent and remaining stay in agreement. An alert fires at 85%.

Phases and tasks

Each event breaks into phases with their own checklists. Tasks carry an owner, a due date and a status, so progress counts finished work instead of guessing.

Bookings end to end

Request, quote, accept, contract, deposit, balance, completion. Six states, each timestamped, so nothing important was agreed in a chat you cannot find.

A team with real roles

Managers, coordinators and assistants each get the access their job needs and see only the events they are assigned to.

A client portal

Share progress, the budget and decisions that need a yes, without handing the client the keys to your workspace or your cost base.

Messaging in context

Every thread sits against the booking it belongs to, so the quote, the revision and what was agreed stay together.
