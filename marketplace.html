Straight answer

What is free, and what a credit is for

There is no subscription and no card kept on file. You buy credits once and spend them on four actions.

Always free

Browsing the directory. Reading portfolios, price ranges and past events. Messaging a planner before you commit. Viewing your budget, tasks and bookings. A dormant account costs nothing at all.

Costs credits

Creating an event (5). Sending a booking request (5). Inviting someone onto your team (2). Exporting a report (1). That is the whole list, and nothing else draws down your balance.

200 free credits on signup covers roughly 40 events. Credits never expire.

No card, no subscription and no sales call. Create the event and you can be comparing real quotes the same afternoon.
