Our purpose

Mission and vision

Two sentences we are willing to be held to.

Our mission

To become Nigeria’s first all in one event management platform, so professional planning agencies can run their entire operation from a single, well designed tool built for the African market.

Our vision

To be the HoneyBook for Nigeria, and then for the whole of Pan-Africa. A platform where every event professional on the continent has tools that understand their local context, payment rails and culture.

The problem

Most Nigerian planners still run on WhatsApp and Excel

By the company’s own count, around 80% of them. In an industry worth more than $20 billion, with over 55 competing tools, not one was built for Nigerian event planning agencies. We mapped the pain points first, then built against them.

Budget chaos

Tracking spend across ten or more suppliers in spreadsheets, losing money and hours reconciling manual transfers.

Finding a planner you can trust

No checked directory of professionals. Clients rely on WhatsApp recommendations and whoever a friend used last year.

Five to seven disconnected tools

Google Docs, Excel, WhatsApp, email, bank apps and social media, with no single source of truth.

Payment tracking

Manual transfers, no receipts and no structured invoices. Clients dispute payments and there is no audit trail.

No communication trail

Client briefs live in WhatsApp DMs. When a dispute arrives, there is no documented history of decisions or approvals.

Nothing built for here

Fifty five competing tools, and not one designed around naira, local bank rails or the way Nigerian agencies actually work.

What makes it different

Local first

Built from the ground up for Nigeria: naira payments, local bank integrations and an interface that matches how Nigerian agencies already work.

Comprehensive
