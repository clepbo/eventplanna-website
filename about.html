Our purpose

Mission and vision

Two sentences we are willing to be held to.

Our mission

To become Nigeria’s first all in one event management platform, so professional planning agencies can run their entire operation from a single, well designed tool built for the African market.

Our vision

To be the HoneyBook for Nigeria, and then for the whole of Pan-Africa. A platform where every event professional on the continent has tools that understand their local context, payment rails and culture.

The problem

Most Nigerian planners still run on WhatsApp and Excel

By the company’s own count, around 80% of them. In an industry worth more than $20 billion, with over 55 competing tools, not one was built for Nigerian event planning agencies. We mapped the pain points first, then built against them.

Where we work

Built in Nigeria, for Nigeria

A sharp focus on the commercial heartbeats of the country and, after that, the continent. Naira pricing, local bank rails and local supplier culture, because that is what the work actually runs on.

Local first

Naira payments, local bank integrations, and an interface that matches how Nigerian agencies already work.

Comprehensive

Replace seven tools with one: clients, budgets, bookings, payments, timelines and team.

Accessible pricing

Credits, not an eye watering monthly fee, so a solo planner and a large agency reach the same tools.

Mobile first

Nigerian planners work on their phones, so the product is designed for a phone and fully works on one.

AI where it earns it

Budget suggestions, planner matching and timelines trained on Nigerian market data, not US defaults.
