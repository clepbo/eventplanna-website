About

Events are built by people.
The software should help them find each other.

We started EventPlanna because the hardest part of an event was never the event. It was finding people you could trust, and keeping track of what everyone agreed.

Ask any planner in Lagos how they source a caterer and you will hear the same answer: a referral, a phone call, and hope. Ask a client how they found their planner and it is usually Instagram.

That works right up until it doesn't — the vendor who ghosts two weeks out, the quote that quietly changed, the budget nobody has reconciled since March.

So we built both halves: a marketplace where the people are verified, and a workspace where the agreements live.

What we care about

Three things we won't trade

Verification before volume

It would be quicker to let anyone list. We check ID, business registration and address first, because a marketplace is only worth anything if the badge means something.

Pay for use, not for access

Most event businesses are seasonal. Charging a monthly fee through a quiet quarter is a tax on a bad month, so we charge for actions instead and let dormant accounts cost nothing.

Built for Naira, built for here

Nigerian pricing, Nigerian payment rails, Nigerian payout cycles. Not a template localised after the fact.
