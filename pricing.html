Pricing

No subscription. You pay for actions.

Event work is seasonal, so a monthly fee through a quiet quarter is a tax on a bad month. Buy credits once, spend them on four actions, and let the account sit idle for free when there is nothing on.

What a credit buys

Four actions draw down your balance

Nothing else on the platform costs a credit. Browsing the marketplace, reading portfolios, messaging a vendor and viewing your own budget are free, always.

Create an event 5

Builds the workspace from the event type: phases, a starter checklist, a category budget and a client portal.

Booking request 5

Carries the date, headcount and budget to the vendor, so what comes back is a priced quote rather than a question.

Invite a team member 2

Adds a coordinator or assistant with their own role, permissions and assigned events. Charged once, not monthly.

Export a report 1

A client-ready budget or progress pack, recording the figures as they stood at the moment you exported.

Planners

Planners do not pay to be listed

Listing is free

Applying, being reviewed, appearing in the directory and receiving briefs all cost nothing. Replying to a brief with a quote costs nothing either, because the credit was spent by whoever sent it.

Payment processing, at cost

Deposits and balances are paid in-app. Card payments carry the gateway fee plus VAT on that fee, itemised before anyone confirms; bank transfer is free. Funds are released once delivery is confirmed.

Prices are in Nigerian naira and include VAT where it applies. Planners are paid in naira to a verified business account.
