Planners

Planners do not pay to be listed

Being listed is free. The only money that reaches us from a planner is a share of a paid consultation.

Listing is free

Applying, being reviewed, appearing in the directory and receiving briefs all cost nothing. Replying to a brief with a quote costs nothing either, because the credit was spent by whoever sent it.

Consultation fee, 1.5%

Where a planner charges for a consultation, EventPlanna takes 1.5% of that fee. That is our share only. The payment provider’s own charges sit on top and are paid by the client when they pay for the consultation.

Payment processing, at cost

Deposits and balances are paid in-app through a payment provider. Whichever method the client picks, the provider’s charge and the VAT on it are passed through at cost and itemised before anyone confirms. Funds are released once delivery is confirmed.
