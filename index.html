Why EventPlanna

Not a directory. Not a spreadsheet. The sourcing and the running of the event, in one system that both sides can see.

See pricing

Matched, not listed

Briefs are filtered by budget, date, city and event type — so clients see vendors who are actually free and in range, and vendors stop reading briefs they were never going to win.

Verified before they're visible

Government ID, business registration and proof of address are checked before a vendor can take a booking. The badge means something.

The money is never a mystery

Every quote, deposit and balance lands against a budget line. You always know what is committed, what is paid and what is still open.

Pay as you go, not per seat

Credits are spent on the things that cost us money to run — creating an event, sending a booking request, inviting a teammate. Browsing and planning are always free.

Your whole team, properly

Managers, coordinators and assistants each get the access they should have — and only see the events they're actually on.
