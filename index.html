The workspace

What sits behind a booking

Finding the vendor is the visible part. Most of what decides whether an event runs smoothly happens afterwards — and that is usually where the thread goes cold.

Budgets that reconcile

Every quote, deposit and balance lands against a budget line. Allocated, spent and remaining are always the same three numbers on everyone’s screen.

Phases and tasks

Events break into phases with their own checklists. Tasks carry an owner, a due date and a status, and the progress bar is a count, not a feeling.

Bookings end to end

Request, quote, accept, contract, deposit, balance. Each booking moves through the same states and nothing is agreed in a chat nobody can find later.

A team with real roles

Managers, coordinators and assistants each get the access they should have, and only see the events they are assigned to.

A client portal

Share progress, budget and decisions with the client through a link, without giving them the keys to your workspace.

Messaging in context

Every conversation sits against the booking it belongs to, so the quote, the revision and what was agreed stay together.
